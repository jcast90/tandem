# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Tandem contributors should make participation respectful, useful, and safe for
everyone. Be constructive, assume good intent while responding to impact, and
welcome people regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving actionable feedback, acknowledging
tradeoffs, respecting privacy, and helping newcomers understand project
conventions. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention,
doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, project
spaces, and public situations where someone represents Tandem. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith. Maintainers
who are involved in a report should recuse themselves from deciding it.
